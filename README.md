# Sovereign Sales OS — 24/7 Autonomous Sales Department

An on-premise, sovereign AI sales appliance coordinating seven specialized autonomous agents running 24/7. Built on the sovereign multi-agent architecture blueprint.

## ⚡ The 7 Autonomous Agents Mapped
1. **01 · CSO & Lead Router** — Deterministic + LLM hybrid orchestrator evaluating leads against `icp-matrix.md`.
2. **02 · Prospecting & Deep Research** — Enriches decision-maker dossiers via Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Nav & Crunchbase.
3. **03 · Multichannel Cold Outreach** — Hyper-personalized outbound messaging via Smartlead and Gmail warm relay.
4. **04 · Inbound SDR & Qualification** — 38-second BANT/MEDDPICC qualification via webhooks.
5. **05 · Call Logistics & Booker** — Calendar coordination via Cal.com + executive pre-call briefings in Slack.
6. **06 · Closer Co-Pilot (YOU) 🟢** — The sovereign human guard: required founder approval for pricing and discounts.
7. **07 · RevOps & CRM Auto-Sync** — Automated CRM transition in HubSpot, Stripe invoice generation, and Slack onboarding.
